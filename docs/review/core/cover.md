## Strengths
- Honest-by-construction assessment contract: parse gate, testMatch fit, polarity inversion, vacuous-intent, TODO triage, locator/value grounding funnel into one result with a clear blocked/needsReview/groundingDriven split shared with oneshot/repair.
- The @raiken-unverified stamp is keyed to grounding-driven reasons only and raiken test refuses stamped specs — the false-green prevention reasoning is unusually well articulated (assess-draft.ts:163-170, draft-quality.ts:59-70).
- Degrade-to-empty discipline: missing DBs/graphs never make cover worse; the cold-start gate refuses to invent rather than drafting blind; the signed-out-knowledge re-crawl closes a real hole without blocking.
- Strong anti-false-green repair guards (proven-absent vs proven-present locators, weakened/dropped expectations, value-mismatch treated as app-side bug) with the why documented inline.
- Clobber protection including the subtle steering case; --fix-config widens before steering so the guards can't deadlock (tested).
